# Logos

`tripami-wordmark.webp`: the Tripami wordmark, as supplied. Navy `brand-navy` (#02203A) letters with the `brand-orange` (#FC6316) gradient dot on the i, on a transparent ground (2000×667, artwork 1646×480 inside).

- Place it on light grounds only: `canvas`, `surface` or `logo-ground`. There is no reversed (light-on-dark) version yet; on dark screens, set it on a `logo-ground` plate with `radius-lg` and `space-6` padding.
- Keep clear space of at least the dot's diameter on every side. Never recolor, outline, stretch or re-typeset it, and never separate the dot.
- The orange dot is the only place `brand-orange` appears at full strength in product UI.
