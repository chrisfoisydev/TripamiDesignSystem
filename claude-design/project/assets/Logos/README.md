# Logos

Two versions of the Tripami wordmark, as supplied. Both are on a transparent ground (2000×667).

- `tripami-wordmark.webp`: `brand-navy` (#02203A) letters with the `brand-orange` (#FC6316) dot. Use it on light grounds: `canvas`, `surface`, `logo-ground`, light photos.
- `tripami-wordmark-reversed.webp`: white letters with the same orange dot. Use it on dark grounds: dark-theme `canvas` and `surface`, `brand-navy`, and dark or scrimmed photos.

- Pick the version by the ground, never by the theme alone: a light photo in dark mode still takes the navy version.
- Keep clear space of at least the dot's diameter on every side. Never recolor, outline, stretch or re-typeset it, and never separate the dot.
- The orange dot is the only place `brand-orange` appears at full strength in product UI.
